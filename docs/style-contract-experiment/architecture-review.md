In October 2026, the facilities team will replace two rooftop heating and cooling units at the North Campus library.

The work requires a county mechanical permit and two inspections. The project budget is $84,000.

The library will remain open during the project, but the east entrance will close for three days. The project manager must publish the inspection schedule and notify library staff at least 48 hours before each closure.

If weather delays the crane work, the facilities team will move the installation to the next available Saturday.
